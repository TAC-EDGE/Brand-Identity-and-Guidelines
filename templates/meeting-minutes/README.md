# TACEDGE meeting minutes template v1.0

Status: provisional (D10) · Owner: Mike Coom · Updated: 2026-07-15
Fonts: Play, Be Vietnam Pro, JetBrains Mono (install from Google Fonts; not distributed). Office fallbacks: Arial / Consolas.
Regenerate outputs with the scripts in /scripts — never edit exported PDFs or previews by hand.

## Files
- `TACEDGE_Meeting_Minutes_Template_v1.0.docx` — editable source (A4).
- `TACEDGE_Meeting_Minutes_Template_v1.0.pdf` + `previews/` — rendered example.

## Rules
- Capture what was decided, why it matters and who acts next. Never a transcript.
- Action statuses: Open · In progress · Complete · Blocked (name the blocker).
- Discussion: three sentences per topic at most.
